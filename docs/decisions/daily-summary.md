# The daily summary is in the app, not in email (P2-11)

**Decided 6 October 2026.**

What is due today shows on Today, at the top of "Coming up", with how many
tasks are overdue or due today and a link to the full due list. Nothing is
emailed.

Why: she opens the tool to work anyway, and a daily email that repeats what the
first screen already says is one more thing to ignore. An email also needs a
sending service, an unsubscribe, and a decision about what happens when the
summary is wrong, none of which earn their place before the tool is in daily
use.

What would change this: if she goes days without opening the tool and misses
dates because of it, a morning email becomes worth building. The due list is
already a cross-project query, so sending it is a small piece of work on top.
